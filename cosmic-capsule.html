The Cosmic Time Capsule

A Micro-Art & Eternal Light Particle Experiment

Imagine trapping a flash of light inside your own artwork, and then releasing it to travel through the freezing void of deep space forever. That is exactly what you are creating today.

By painting a custom message with special luminescent ink, your card acts as a physical time capsule. Best of all, you can mail your physical art capsule to fly to space aboard a real spacecraft!

⚡ Creation Protocol

  1. Design Your Canvas: Use your glow pen to sign your initials, write a poem, or draw a space ship on one side of a standard 4x6 inch postcard or cardstock. Keep the back clean for mailing data.
  2. Optical Core Charge: Hold your finished card directly beneath a bright desk lamp or phone flashlight for 60 seconds. This packs the ink molecules full of raw energy.
  3. Launch the Photon Stream: Step outside under a clear night sky. Point your flashlight beam straight up into the stars. Trillions of light particles are escaping into the cosmos instantly.
⏱️ Pre-Flight Kindness Mission: While your ink charges, pay your energy forward by doing a quiet favor or sending a text message of true appreciation to a friend or family member on Earth.

🚀 Launch Pad Mission

You can turn this physical demonstration into a genuine spaceflight mission! Put your finished card into an envelope and mail it directly to Blue Origin's payload facility. They will load your postcard canvas into a real space capsule, fly it past the edge of Earth's atmosphere into zero-gravity, and mail it back to you officially stamped "Flown to Space"!
