# 🛰️ THE MOORE SIGNAL ARCHIVE
### Creative STEM Education & Deep-Space Simulation Suite
*Conceived by Angela N. Moore • Developed in Collaboration with Gemini AI*

---

### 🎨 About the Archive
The Moore Signal Archive is an open-source project that blends deep-space science, contemporary poetry, and real-world geocaching games into interactive learning experiences. 

Our broader interdisciplinary initiatives were featured publicly during **[CT Tech Week](https://cttechweek.com/)** at the Ely Center for Contemporary Art. All shared components are globally accessible under our **[CC BY 4.0 Legal License](https://creativecommons.org)**.

* 🎟️ **Request an Interactive Exhibit:** [bio.site/mooresignal](https://bio.site/mooresignal)
* 🌌 **Interactive 3D Galaxy Map:** [cyber-chic.github.io/cosmos/universe](https://cyber-chic.github.io/cosmos/universe)

### 🚀 Hands-On Learning Labs & Simulations
These open-source, student-friendly simulations demonstrate how real space concepts work using light, simple electronics, and interactive projects.

| Activity / Lab | The Science Concept | Try the Digital Simulation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ALPHA** <br> *1-Min Lightsail* | **Sailing on Light:** See how spacecraft can travel through deep space using the push of light particles (photons) instead of heavy chemical fuel. | 🌌 [Launch Experience](https://cyber-chic.github.io/cosmos/spacecraft) |
| **BETA** <br> *10-Sec Photon Rocket* | **Action & Reaction:** A kinetic thrust simulation. Learn physics fundamentals using student-built LED circuits that demonstrate propulsion via light. | 🌌 [Launch Experience](https://cyber-chic.github.io/cosmos/rockets) <br><br> ⚠️ **Safety:** *Lithium coin-cell hazard. Supervise strictly; keep away from children/pets.* |
| **GAMMA** <br> *Invisible Ink Capsule* | **Hidden Data:** Examine how light wavelengths can reveal secrets. Uses UV light and glowing phosphors to demonstrate chemical reactions. | 🌌 [Launch Experience](https://cyber-chic.github.io/cosmos/cosmic-capsule) |
