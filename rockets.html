The 10-Second Photon Rocket

Educational STEM Space Demonstration

Imagine holding a solid-state rocket assembly that functions without combusting fire, smoke, or physical fuel loads. Instead, it discharges trillions of light particles—known as photons—directly into space.

Astrophysicists are actively researching macro-scale Photon Propulsion systems to drive deep-space probes toward distant star systems at light speed. Today, you will build a basic educational model to trace this concept. Assembly requires three items, zero complex circuits, and 10 seconds.

Assembly Protocol

Step 1
Terminal Verification

Examine the LED bulb frame. Identify the two raw metal wire pins, noting that one side extends longer than the other.

Step 2
Core Insertion

Position the flat coin battery cleanly directly between the open paths of the two metal wire terminals.

Step 3
Circuit Ignition

Compress the longer terminal wire leg flush against the smooth positive (+) casing layer. Snap the shorter leg against the opposite bumpy side. The circuit initializes instant light discharge.

Step 4
Structural Core Lock

Wind a layer of utility tape tightly around the completed module layout. This maintains steady terminal contact pressure so the device glows independently.

Testing: With an adult supervisor, this assembly can be taken outside into a clear night environment. Aim your module upward into the open sky—your projected photon stream travels at 186,000 miles per second, exiting the atmosphere into deep space within a fraction of a millisecond.

[ Technical Footnotes & Safety Protocols ]

01 / Ingestion Hazard: This assembly relies on unsealed lithium coin cell components. If swallowed, lithium core cells react with internal fluids to cause severe, fatal tissue burns in under 2 hours. Keep all loose components locked entirely away from infants, toddlers, and pets.

02 / Mandatory Supervision: This STEM activity must only be handled under the direct, continuous monitoring of a parent or adult supervisor. Once contact is verified, an adult should immediately encase the battery and terminal components completely in heavy-duty duct tape to prevent anyone from separating or swallowing the parts.

03 / Electrical Status: The standalone 3V battery component is completely safe to touch with bare hands and does not pose a shock hazard. Under no circumstances should you attempt to plug loose LED components or wire connections into household electrical wall outlets.
